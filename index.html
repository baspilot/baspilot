FAQ
Does BAS Pilot lodge my BAS with the ATO?↓
No. BAS Pilot is a calculation tool only. You lodge yourself via the ATO Business Portal, myGov, or through your registered tax agent.
Is my bank data safe?↓
Yes. All file processing happens entirely in your browser. Your bank statements are never uploaded to our servers at any point.
Which banks are supported?↓
Any Australian bank that exports CSV or Excel — CommBank, ANZ, Westpac, NAB, Bendigo, ING, and more.
How accurate is the AI?↓
Around 90–95% for common transactions. You should always review before using the BAS figures, especially for unusual or mixed-use transactions.
Can I cancel anytime?↓
Yes. Cancel from Account Settings. Your subscription remains active until end of billing period. Full refund within 7 days of first payment.

About BAS Pilot

We built the tool we wished existed when running our own businesses — a fast, smart, no-nonsense way to handle GST without paying $300 to an accountant every quarter.

🇦🇺 Built for Australia
BAS Pilot is built specifically for the Australian tax system. We understand CommBank export formats, ATO rulings, GST-free supplies, and the reality of sole trader life. Every feature is designed around Australian business needs — not adapted from a US product.
🤖 How the AI Works
Our AI uses pattern recognition to match transaction descriptions against 30+ business categories. It's trained on thousands of Australian bank transactions — Ampol, Telstra, CommBank, ATO BPAY, Woolworths, Facebook Ads, and more. It runs entirely in your browser — your data never leaves your device.
⚖️ Our Commitment
We're transparent about what BAS Pilot is — a software tool, not a registered tax agent. We always recommend verifying with a professional for complex situations. We charge fair prices, never lock you in, and never sell your data.
